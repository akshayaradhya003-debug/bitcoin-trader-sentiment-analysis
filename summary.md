Methodology

The project combines trader transaction data with the Bitcoin Fear and Greed Index. Data preprocessing included timestamp conversion, merging datasets by date, and generating key trading metrics such as PnL, win rate, and trading frequency.

Insights

Trader performance tends to improve during Greed market conditions.

Trading activity and position sizes increase during positive market sentiment.

Consistent traders maintain better profitability compared to inconsistent traders.

Strategy Recommendations

During Fear market conditions, traders should reduce position sizes and trade cautiously.

During Greed periods, traders can increase trading activity and focus more on long positions.
